# Adopters

This file names parties that have **emitted a real ACR under their own key** —
not "integrated with corrlog," not "interested," but actually produced a signed,
independently-verifiable correction receipt in the wild.

The list is empty on purpose. corrlog's only meaningful adoption metric is an
external key signing a real failure. Stars, forks, and "we added a wrapper" are
not adoption.

The bar for a name on this list:

- the party is **not** the corrlog maintainer or its own test suite;
- they emitted at least one ACR signed by a key they control;
- that receipt verifies against the standalone verifier (`verifier/standalone.py`)
  under their pinned public key.
